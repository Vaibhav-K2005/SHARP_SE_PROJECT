# Journal Entry – Mess Feedback Feature

## Work Done

Worked on the mess feedback feature for the SHARP Student Portal and Mess Manager Portal.

## Contributions

- Added a student form to submit feedback for a selected meal and category.
- Added star ratings and written comments to feedback submissions.
- Added a student-facing view of recent feedback.
- Added a manager-facing feedback view to review submissions for the assigned mess.
- Kept the reporter's identity out of the feedback data presented to mess managers.
- Added filters and rating summaries to help managers review feedback.
- Tested feedback submission, rating validation, mess scoping, and anonymity in manager responses.

## Outcome

Students can submit categorized meal feedback, and mess managers can review feedback for their assigned mess without seeing the reporter's identity.
